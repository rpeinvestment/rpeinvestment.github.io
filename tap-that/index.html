Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

Tap That: Reflex Game ("the App") is developed by Erwin Labs. This policy describes how information is collected and used when you use the App.

Information We Collect

The App itself does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. Your game data, including your personal best score, is stored locally on your device and is not sent to any server.

Third-Party Advertising

The App displays ads provided by third-party advertising services, including Google AdMob. These services may collect and use certain information to serve relevant ads, including:

— Device identifiers (such as advertising ID)
— Usage data and app interaction data
— Diagnostic and performance data

This data is collected and processed by the advertising provider, not by Erwin Labs. For more information on how Google uses your data, visit: Google Privacy Policy.

App Tracking Transparency

On iOS, the App requests your permission via App Tracking Transparency before any data is used for ad tracking. You can change this permission at any time in your device settings.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
